## 📌 Project Overview

The **Andhra Bank Credit Card Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive
Microsoft Power BI dashboard developed to analyze customer demographics,
credit card spending, transaction behavior, income, payment methods,
spending categories, occupations, and city-wise spending patterns.

The dashboard transforms customer and transaction data into meaningful
KPIs, interactive visualizations, demographic insights, spending analysis,
and geographic analysis.

It provides a centralized analytical view that helps users explore
customer spending behavior and transaction patterns across different
segments.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze credit card customer
and transaction data and identify meaningful patterns related to spending,
income, transactions, demographics, payment methods, and customer behavior.

The dashboard focuses on:

👥 Customer demographics

💳 Payment methods

💰 Customer spending

💵 Customer income

🔄 Transaction behavior

🛒 Spending categories

🎂 Age groups

⚧️ Gender

💼 Occupation

🏙️ City-wise spending

📅 Monthly analysis

💍 Marital status

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 💳 Payment Type Analysis

The dashboard analyzes total spending across different payment methods.

The payment methods include:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI
- Net Banking

This visualization helps compare the contribution of different payment
methods to overall customer spending.

---

### 🎂 Age Group & Gender Analysis

The dashboard compares total spending across different age groups
and gender segments.

The analysis includes:

- Age group-wise spending
- Female spending
- Male spending
- Comparison between age groups
- Gender-based spending patterns

Age groups represented in the dashboard include:

- 21–24
- 25–34
- 35–45
- 45+

---

### 🏙️ City-wise Spending Analysis

A map visualization is used to analyze the geographic distribution
of customer spending.

The analysis helps explore:

- City-wise spending
- Geographic customer activity
- Regional spending patterns
- Concentration of customer transactions

---

### 👥 Gender-wise Spending Analysis

The dashboard provides a gender-wise breakdown of total spending.

The visualization helps compare:

- Male spending
- Female spending
- Percentage contribution by gender

---

### 🛒 Category-wise Spending Analysis

The dashboard analyzes total spending across different spending categories.

The categories include:

- Bills
- Groceries
- Electronics
- Health & Wellness
- Travel
- Food
- Entertainment
- Apparel
- Others

This analysis helps identify the categories contributing to overall
customer spending.

---

### 💼 Occupation-wise Income Analysis

The dashboard compares average customer income across different
occupational groups.

The analysis includes:

- Business Owners
- Salaried IT Employees
- Government Employees
- Salaried Other Employees
- Freelancers

This provides an overview of income differences across customer
occupation segments.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard contains interactive slicers that allow users to
explore different customer and transaction segments.

Available filters include:

- 📅 Month
- 💳 Payment Type
- 🏙️ City
- ⚧️ Gender
- 💍 Marital Status
- 💼 Occupation
- 🛒 Category

Users can combine multiple filters to perform focused analysis
of specific customer segments.
